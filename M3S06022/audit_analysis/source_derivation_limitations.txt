The retained SOFA derivations use pragmatic cross-database approximations. Respiratory SOFA combines the minimum PaO2 and maximum FiO2 within each 24-hour window, rather than time-matched arterial gas and oxygen measurements. Missing components contribute zero to the total and four-system SOFA scores; component-specific missing values may subsequently be carried forward or imputed as model predictors, so the two operations serve different purposes. For eICU, first-day APACHE variables provide fallback values in W1 only. The eICU vasopressor extraction explicitly converts rates labelled mcg/min using recorded weight; other recorded rate conventions are not systematically converted in the retained code. The effect of such unit heterogeneity has not been quantified. These features and treatment-related SOFA components can affect comparability between databases. Predictor windows are defined by clinical event/chart timestamps rather than a verified timestamp of availability to a prospective clinician; the analysis therefore does not establish real-time availability of every predictor at the landmark. In MIMIC-IV, the demographic age variable is anchor_age as retained in the supplied derivation, not an independently reconstructed admission age. These limitations require validation of the extraction rules and recalibration before prospective use.
